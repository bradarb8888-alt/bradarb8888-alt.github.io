Freehold: Mortgage Payoff Planner
Privacy Policy

Short version: Freehold doesn't collect, store on a server, share or sell any information about you. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores

The loan details you type in (a nickname, balance, original amount, interest rate, monthly payment, next payment month and any extra-payment plan) and your light/dark preference are saved in the app's private storage on your device. Nothing else is saved.

What the app sends

Nothing. Freehold does not request the Internet permission, so it has no way to send data off your device. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking code, and it has no accounts or sign-in.

Permissions

Freehold requests no runtime or dangerous permissions. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, files or photos.

Exporting a schedule

If you tap "Export as CSV", the app creates a spreadsheet file in its own temporary storage and opens Android's share sheet. You choose where the file goes, such as email or a cloud drive. That transfer is handled by the app you pick, under that app's privacy policy.

Backups

Freehold opts out of Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so your loan details are not copied to Google's backup service.

Deleting your data

Delete a loan inside the app, clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app. Any of these removes the data permanently. There is no copy anywhere else.

Children

Freehold is a general-audience finance calculator and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Not financial advice

Freehold is a calculator. Its results are estimates based on the numbers you enter and are not financial advice.
