Downhill Roll
Privacy Policy

Short version: No account, no ads, no analytics. Your location is used on the device to guide you. It isn't stored or sent to us. To plan a route, the start and finish points are sent to the map services listed below.

Location

Used while the app is open to show where you are and to use your position as the start. While you're rolling, it's also used with the screen off to give turn cues; a notification shows whenever this is happening. Location is never saved to history.

Map services

Route planning sends the start, finish and a few points along the way to Mapbox (directions) and Open-Meteo (elevation). Place search sends the text you type, and an approximate map position to rank nearby results, to Mapbox. Map images are requested from Mapbox for the area on screen. These providers receive your IP address as with any internet request. No name, account, advertising ID or device ID is sent. See mapbox.com/legal/privacy and open-meteo.com for their policies.

What the app stores

Saved places, recent finishes and settings, in the app's private storage. Map images are cached on the device for up to 30 days.

What the developer receives

Nothing. The app has no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking code.

Permissions

Location (precise or approximate) for guidance. Foreground service and notifications, so guidance continues with the screen off and can be stopped from the notification. Vibration for cue buzzes. Internet for maps, search, routes and elevation.

Backups

The app opts out of Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

Deleting your data

Remove saved places and recents in the Places tab. Uninstalling removes everything, including cached map images.

Children

Downhill Roll is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date, and the in-app Privacy screen will be updated in the next release.
