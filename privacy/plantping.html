PlantPing: Water Reminder - Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

PlantPing: Water Reminder is a plant watering reminder published by Free The People Apps ("we"). This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

Data stored on your device

Plant names, schedules and photos are stored only on your device. We do not receive them.

We do not require an account, and we do not sell personal data.

Permissions

  • Camera: optional, to take a photo of a plant. Photos are stored only on your device.
  • Notifications, exact alarms, run at startup, foreground service: deliver watering reminders on time, including after a restart.
  • Internet and network state: loads ads.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob for ads (see below).

Advertising (Google AdMob)

This app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device and app information, and ad interaction data. The app does not currently show its own ad-consent screen, so ads may be personalized based on your advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads; on Amazon Fire tablets, Settings > Privacy > Interest-based Ads). Google's use of data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Third parties

Google AdMob (ads). These providers handle data under their own privacy policies.

Data retention and deletion

Data on your device is kept until you delete it in the app, clear the app's data (Settings > Apps > PlantPing: Water Reminder > Storage > Clear data), or uninstall the app. We hold no data about you on our servers. To ask about deleting ad data held by Google, use Google's controls listed above. For any other privacy question, contact us.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. Ads are requested as non-child-directed.

Changes

We will post updates on this page and change the effective date.
