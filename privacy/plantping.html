PlantPing
Privacy Policy

Short version: PlantPing doesn't collect, upload, share or sell anything. Your plants and watering history stay on your phone.

What the app stores

The plants you add (nickname, type, room, light, pot size, watering interval, feeding schedule, notes), the dates you water and feed them, and your settings. Everything is kept in the app's private storage on your device.

What the app sends

Nothing. PlantPing does not request the Internet permission. It has no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking code, and no accounts.

Permissions

Notifications: one reminder on days a plant needs water, at the time you choose. Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): to set that reminder again after a restart. PlantPing does not access your location, camera, photos, contacts or files.

Backups

PlantPing opts out of Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

Deleting your data

Remove any plant in the app. Uninstalling PlantPing or clearing its storage removes everything.

Children

PlantPing is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date, and the in-app Privacy screen will be updated in the next release.
