Glint - Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

Glint is a hidden-camera and nearby-device check app published by Direct Apps ("we"). It comes in two editions: Glint (free, with ads) and Glint Pro (no ads, no ads SDK). This policy explains what data each edition uses and why.

Data stored on your device

Your check history and settings are stored only in the app's storage on your phone. We do not receive them. Reports leave your phone only when you tap Share. We do not require an account, and we do not sell personal data.

Permissions

  • Camera: frames are analysed in memory to find bright reflections, then discarded. Nothing is photographed, recorded or uploaded.
  • Wi-Fi, network state and multicast: talks only to devices on the Wi-Fi you are connected to, to identify them. In the free edition, internet access is also used to load ads and the consent form.
  • Nearby Wi-Fi devices and Bluetooth scan: lists nearby hotspots and Bluetooth devices during a check. Declared "never for location".
  • Location (Android 12 and older only): Android requires it on older versions to list Wi-Fi and Bluetooth devices. Glint never reads or stores your location.
  • USB: used only when you connect a USB device to the phone and choose to use it with Glint.
  • Advertising ID (free edition only): used by Google AdMob for ads. Glint Pro does not request it.

Advertising (Google AdMob, free edition only)

The free edition shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Ads may be personalized where you have given consent. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android settings. Google's use of data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Glint Pro shows no ads.

Consent

In regions where consent is required (such as the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the free edition uses Google's User Messaging Platform, a certified consent tool, to ask for your choice before any ad loads. You can change it any time from "Ad privacy choices" on the app's Privacy screen.

Third parties

Free edition only: Google AdMob (ads) and Google User Messaging Platform (consent). These providers handle data under their own privacy policies.

Data retention and deletion

Data on your device is kept until you delete it in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. We hold no data about you on our servers. To ask about deleting ad data held by Google, use Google's controls above, or contact us.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. Ads are requested as non-child-directed.

Changes

We will post updates on this page and change the effective date.
