PillCheck
Privacy Policy

Short version: PillCheck doesn't collect, upload, share or sell anything. Your medication list and dose history stay on your phone.

What the app stores

The medications you add (name, strength, form, amount, times, days, colour, supply count, notes), the doses you mark taken or skipped, and your settings. All of it is kept in the app's private storage on your device.

What the app sends

Nothing. PillCheck does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere. It has no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking code, and no accounts.

Permissions

Notifications: to remind you when a dose is due. You can turn reminders off in Settings or in Android's settings. Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): so the next reminder is set again after your phone restarts. PillCheck does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Exporting your history

If you tap Export history, PillCheck builds a CSV of the last 90 days and opens Android's share sheet. You choose the app it goes to, such as email. That transfer is handled by the app you choose, under its own privacy policy.

Backups

PillCheck opts out of Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so your health information is not copied to a backup service.

Deleting your data

Delete any medication inside the app. Uninstalling PillCheck, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes everything permanently.

Medical disclaimer

PillCheck is a reminder and logbook. It is not medical advice and does not check doses, interactions or side effects. Follow your prescriber's and pharmacist's instructions.

Children

PillCheck is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date, and the in-app Privacy screen will be updated in the next release.
