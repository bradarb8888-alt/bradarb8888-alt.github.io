PillCheck Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026 (version 1.1.0)
Publisher: Free The People Apps
Contact: bradarb8888@gmail.com

Summary

PillCheck is free and shows ads provided by Start.io. Your medication list, dose times and history are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us, to Start.io or to anyone else, and we (Free The People Apps) cannot see them. There is no account and no analytics. The app uses the internet only to load ads.

Information you enter

PillCheck stores the following only in a private database on your device:

  • Medication names, optional dose notes, and the times you set for reminders.
  • A log of which doses you marked as taken, and when.
  • Your settings (for example, whether reminders are on).

This information may be health-related. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, it is never shared with the ad network, and it is not sold or used for advertising. Ads are not targeted based on your medications.

Permissions

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): used only to show your dose reminders. You can deny or turn this off at any time in the app's Settings or in Android settings.
  • Run at startup, wake lock, foreground service: added automatically by Android's WorkManager library so reminders survive restarts and run efficiently.
  • Internet, network and Wi-Fi state, advertising ID (AD_ID), install referrer, ad topics: added by the Start.io advertising SDK and used only to load, show and measure ads.
  • PillCheck does not request GPS location, contacts, camera, storage, or exact-alarm permissions.

Advertising (Start.io)

PillCheck shows a small banner ad on the Meds and History screens. Ads never appear on the Today screen, on dose reminders, or as full-screen ads. Ads are served by Start.io (Start.io Inc., formerly StartApp). To show and measure ads, the Start.io SDK collects:

  • your device's advertising ID (you can reset it or delete it in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads),
  • device information such as model, operating system version, language, screen size and mobile carrier,
  • your IP address, and approximate (coarse) location derived from it,
  • ad interaction data such as ad views and clicks.

Start.io handles this data under its own privacy policy: https://www.start.io/policy/privacy-policy/. Your medication data is never shared with Start.io.

Your ad choice (consent)

The first time you open PillCheck, it asks whether you allow personalized ads. No ad SDK is started until you answer.

  • Yes, personalize: Start.io may use the data above to show ads based on your interests.
  • No thanks: you still see ads, but they are not personalized. We tell Start.io that you have not consented (GDPR) and that you opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information (US privacy string, CCPA/CPRA).

You can change your choice anytime in PillCheck > Settings > Ad privacy choices.

Other third parties

PillCheck contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. Start.io is the only third party that receives data from the app.

Backups

If you have turned on Android's device backup, Android may include PillCheck's data in your own Google backup under your account and Google's terms. Free The People Apps has no access to these backups.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can remove a medication in the app, clear all data in Android Settings > Apps > PillCheck > Storage > Clear data, or uninstall the app, which deletes everything.

Children

PillCheck is not directed at children under 13 (16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Medical disclaimer

PillCheck is a reminder and log tool. It does not give medical advice. Reminders may be delayed by Android's battery saving. Always follow your doctor's or pharmacist's instructions.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and, for significant changes, note them in the app's release notes.
