PillCheck Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

This policy covers both PillCheck apps on Google Play: PillCheck (free, with ads; com.directapps.pillcheck) and PillCheck Premium (no ads; com.directapps.pillcheck.premium).

Your medication data

Medications, schedules, and dose history are stored only on your device in a local database. We have no servers and never receive, sell, or share this data. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising (free version only)

The free version shows banner ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (including the Android Advertising ID), IP address, and ad interaction data to serve, measure, and (where permitted) personalize ads. See How Google uses data from partner apps. PillCheck Premium contains no ads, no ad SDK, and does not access the Advertising ID.

Consent

Where required by law (e.g. EEA, UK, Switzerland, and certain US states), the free version asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time from the app's privacy options. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android settings.

Permissions

Notifications are used only to remind you to take doses. Internet access is used only by the ad SDK in the free version.

Children

PillCheck is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We will update this page and the effective date if this policy changes.
