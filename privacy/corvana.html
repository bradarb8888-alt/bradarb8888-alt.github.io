Corvana - Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

Developer: Free The People Apps, Canada. Contact: bradarb8888@gmail.com

Corvana comes in two editions:

  • Corvana Pro (com.directapps.corvana.pro) does not collect, store or share any of your data. It has no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no Google Play Services or Firebase.
  • Corvana (free, com.directapps.corvana) works the same way, except that it shows banner ads from Google AdMob. Google collects the ad-related data described below. We still collect nothing ourselves.

What Corvana accesses

Corvana asks for access to your device storage so it can show and manage your files. Your files are read and changed only on your device, and only when you ask. Neither edition uploads them, scans them for anyone else, or sends information about them anywhere.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts and Corvana has no servers of its own.

Advertising (free edition only)

The free edition shows ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Ads never use your files or file names. Where consent is required (for example the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform, a certified consent tool, to ask for your choice before any ad loads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android settings. Google's use of data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Corvana Pro contains no advertising code.

Network

Apart from ads in the free edition, Corvana connects to the internet only when you use the network feature to reach a server you entered yourself (for example your own FTP, SFTP, SMB or WebDAV server). That traffic goes directly between your device and that server. Server addresses and login details you save are stored on your device only, encrypted with the Android Keystore.

Vault

Files you move into the vault are encrypted on your device. The key stays on your device. If you forget your PIN and lose biometric access, we cannot recover the files.

Settings and backups

Your settings stay on your device. Corvana opts out of Android cloud backup.

Permissions

  • All files access: to browse and manage files, which is the main job of a file manager.
  • Notifications and foreground service (data sync): to show progress for long copies and transfers and keep them running when you leave the app.
  • Camera: used only when you scan a QR code in the app.
  • Biometrics: used only to unlock the vault.
  • Internet and network state: to reach servers you add, and in the free edition to load ads and the consent form.
  • Advertising ID (free edition only): used by Google AdMob.

Children

Corvana is a general tool and is not aimed at children. Ads in the free edition are requested as non-child-directed.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.
