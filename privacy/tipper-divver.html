Hair Splitter Tipper Divver
Privacy Policy

Short version: The app doesn't collect, upload, share or sell anything. It has no internet access and requests no permissions.

What the app stores

Your settings, and only the checks you choose to save: amounts, tip percentage, the names you typed for a split and an optional place name. They stay in the app's private storage on your device.

What the app sends

Nothing. There is no Internet permission, no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking code, and no account.

Permissions

None.

Sharing

If you tap Share, a text summary is passed to Android's share sheet and goes only to the app you choose.

Backups

The app opts out of Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

Deleting your data

Delete saved checks in the Saved tab. Uninstalling removes everything.

Children

Hair Splitter Tipper Divver is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date, and the in-app Privacy screen will be updated in the next release.
