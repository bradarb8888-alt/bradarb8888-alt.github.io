Mortgage Payoff Date Calculator - Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

Mortgage Payoff Date Calculator is a mortgage and down-payment calculator published by Direct Apps ("we"). This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

Data stored on your device

The loan amounts, rates, ages and payments you enter stay on your device. The app does not ask for your name, an account, or a bank login.

We do not require an account, and we do not sell personal data.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: loads ads in the free version.
  • Advertising ID (free version only): used by Google AdMob for ads (see below). The Premium version does not request it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of this app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Ads may be personalized based on your advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads). Google's use of data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the free version asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are requested, and you can change your choice later. The Premium version (a separate paid app) contains no ads, no AdMob SDK and does not use the advertising ID.

Ad-free version

Mortgage Payoff Premium is sold as a separate paid app on Google Play; Google Play processes the payment and we never see your payment details. The Google Play versions have no in-app purchases.

Third parties

Google AdMob (ads, free version only), Google Play (app purchase and delivery). These providers handle data under their own privacy policies.

Data retention and deletion

Data on your device is kept until you delete it in the app, clear the app's data (Settings > Apps > Mortgage Payoff Date Calculator > Storage > Clear data), or uninstall the app. We hold no data about you on our servers. To ask about deleting ad data held by Google, use Google's controls listed above or contact us.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. Ads are requested as non-child-directed.

Changes

We will post updates on this page and change the effective date.
