FTP DJ – Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

This policy covers both editions of FTP DJ on Google Play: FTP DJ (free, with ads; com.freethepeople.ftpdj) and FTP DJ Pro (no ads; com.freethepeople.ftpdj.pro).

Your music stays on your device

You choose audio files with the Android system file picker. FTP DJ decodes and plays them on your device only. We do not upload, copy to a server, or collect your files, playlists, or mixes. We run no servers and have no user accounts.

FTP DJ Pro (no ads)

The Pro edition has no advertising SDK, no analytics, and no advertising ID permission. It collects and shares no personal data.

FTP DJ free edition (ads)

The free edition shows banner ads from Google AdMob, only on the library and settings screens, and never while a deck is playing or in gig mode. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device identifiers (including the Android advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from IP, and ad interaction and diagnostic data. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Consent

In the EEA, UK, Switzerland, and other places where it is required, the free edition asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are requested. Where no consent is given, ads are not requested. You can reset or limit the advertising ID in Android Settings.

Children

FTP DJ is not directed at children under 13, and its ad requests are not tagged as child-directed.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
