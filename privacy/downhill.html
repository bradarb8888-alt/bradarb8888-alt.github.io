Downhill Roll - Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

Downhill Roll is a downhill route finder for walking, biking and boarding published by Direct Apps ("we"). It comes in two editions: Downhill Roll (free, with ads) and Downhill Roll Pro (paid, no ads, no ads SDK). This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

Data stored on your device

Your location is used live on the device while the app is open. It is not collected in the background and is not stored on our servers.

To draw the map and find routes, the app sends requests to these services: Mapbox (map tiles, place search, and routes; requests include the map area, the text you search, and your start and finish points) and Open-Meteo (elevation lookups for points along the route). These providers receive your IP address with each request and handle it under their own privacy policies (Mapbox, Open-Meteo). We do not receive or keep this data.

We do not require an account, and we do not sell personal data.

Permissions

  • Precise and approximate location: shows your position and sets your start point while the app is open. Location is not collected in the background.
  • Internet and network state: loads maps and routes (and ads in the free edition).
  • Advertising ID (free edition only): used by Google AdMob for ads (see below). Pro does not request it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of this app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Ads may be personalized based on your advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads). Google's use of data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Buying "Remove ads" (below) stops the app from loading ads.

Location

Precise or approximate location is used only while the app is open and on screen to show your position and set route start points. It is sent to Mapbox only as part of map, search and route requests, is not collected in the background, and is never sold or used for ads by us.

Consent (free edition)

Before any ad is requested, the free edition asks for consent where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) using Google's User Messaging Platform, a certified consent tool. Ads are not loaded until consent rules allow it. You can change your choice from the app's privacy options. Downhill Roll Pro shows no ads and contains no ads or consent SDK.

Third parties

Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform (free edition only: ads and consent), Mapbox and Open-Meteo (maps, search, routes, elevation). These providers handle data under their own privacy policies.

Data retention and deletion

Data on your device is kept until you delete it in the app, clear the app's data (Settings > Apps > Downhill Roll > Storage > Clear data), or uninstall the app. We hold no data about you on our servers. To ask about deleting ad data held by Google, use Google's controls listed above.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. Ads are requested as non-child-directed.

Changes

We will post updates on this page and change the effective date.
