Downhill Roll - Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

Downhill Roll is a trail navigation app published by Direct Apps ("we"). This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

Data stored on your device

Your location is used live on the device. It is not stored on our servers, sold, or shared, except that map tile requests reveal the map area being viewed to the map provider.

We do not require an account, and we do not sell personal data.

Permissions

  • Precise and approximate location: shows your position on the trail map while the app is open. Location is not collected in the background.
  • Internet and network state: loads map tiles and ads.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

This app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Ads may be personalized where you have given consent. In regions where consent is required (such as the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your choice before loading personalized ads, and you can change it from the app's settings. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads). Google's use of data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Third parties

Google AdMob (ads). These providers handle data under their own privacy policies.

Data retention and deletion

Data on your device is kept until you delete it in the app, clear the app's data (Settings > Apps > Downhill Roll > Storage > Clear data), or uninstall the app. We hold no data about you on our servers. To ask about deleting ad data held by Google, use Google's controls listed above, or contact us.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. Ads are requested as non-child-directed.

Changes

We will post updates on this page and change the effective date.
