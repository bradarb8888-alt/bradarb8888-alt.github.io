Downhill Roll - Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

Downhill Roll is a downhill route finder for walking, biking and boarding published by Free The People Apps ("we"). This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

Data stored on your device

Your location is used live on the device while the app is open. It is not collected in the background and is not stored on our servers.

To draw the map and find routes, the app sends requests to these services: Mapbox (map tiles, place search, and routes; requests include the map area, the text you search, and your start and finish points) and Open-Meteo (elevation lookups for points along the route). These providers receive your IP address with each request and handle it under their own privacy policies (Mapbox, Open-Meteo). We do not receive or keep this data.

We do not require an account, and we do not sell personal data.

Permissions

  • Precise and approximate location: shows your position and sets your start point while the app is open. Location is not collected in the background.
  • Internet and network state: loads maps, routes and ads.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob for ads (see below).

Advertising (Google AdMob)

This app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device and app information, and ad interaction data. The app does not currently show its own ad-consent screen, so ads may be personalized based on your advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads; on Amazon Fire tablets, Settings > Privacy > Interest-based Ads). Google's use of data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Third parties

Google AdMob (ads), Mapbox and Open-Meteo (maps, search, routes, elevation). These providers handle data under their own privacy policies.

Data retention and deletion

Data on your device is kept until you delete it in the app, clear the app's data (Settings > Apps > Downhill Roll > Storage > Clear data), or uninstall the app. We hold no data about you on our servers. To ask about deleting ad data held by Google, use Google's controls listed above. For any other privacy question, contact us.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. Ads are requested as non-child-directed.

Changes

We will post updates on this page and change the effective date.
