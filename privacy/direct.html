Direct - Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

Direct is a QR and barcode scanner published by Direct Apps ("we"). It comes in two editions: Direct (free, with ads) and Direct Pro (no ads, no ads SDK and no internet permission). This policy explains what data each edition uses and why.

Data stored on your device

Scan history, notes and generated codes are stored only in the app's storage on your device. We do not receive them.

We do not require an account, and we do not sell personal data.

Permissions

  • Camera: reads QR codes and barcodes. Frames are processed on the device and not stored or uploaded.
  • Photos and files: used only when you choose an image to scan or save a generated code.
  • Internet and network state: free edition only: used to load ads and the consent form. Your scans are never sent. Direct Pro has no internet permission.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free edition shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. Direct Pro shows no ads. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Ads may be personalized where you have given consent. In regions where consent is required (such as the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the free edition uses Google's User Messaging Platform (a certified consent tool) to ask for your choice before any ad loads, and you can change it any time from "Ad privacy choices" on the app's Privacy screen. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads). Google's use of data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Third parties

Free edition only: Google AdMob (ads) and Google User Messaging Platform (consent). If Direct Pro is bought, payment is handled by Google Play under Google's privacy policy. These providers handle data under their own privacy policies.

Data retention and deletion

Data on your device is kept until you delete it in the app, clear the app's data (Settings > Apps > Direct > Storage > Clear data), or uninstall the app. We hold no data about you on our servers. To ask about deleting ad data held by Google, use Google's controls listed above, or contact us.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. Ads are requested as non-child-directed.

Changes

We will post updates on this page and change the effective date.
