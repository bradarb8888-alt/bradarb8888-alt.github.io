Privacy Policy

Short version: Direct doesn't collect, upload, share or sell anything. It has no internet access. The camera is used only to read codes while the scan screen is open.

Camera

Used only while the Scan screen is open. Frames are decoded on your device and discarded right away. No photos or video are saved or sent.

Photos

When you scan from a photo, Android's photo picker gives the app only the image you pick. Images shared to Direct from another app are read the same way and not kept. Codes you create are saved to Pictures/Direct only when you tap Save.

What the app stores

Your scan history (the decoded text, code type, time, and any star or note you add) and your settings. They stay in the app's private storage on your device. You can turn history off in Settings.

What the app sends

Nothing. There is no Internet permission, no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking code, and no account.

Permissions

Camera, for live scanning. On Android 9 and older only, storage write access to save a created QR image to your Pictures folder.

Opening and sharing

When you tap Open, Share, Call, Email or Add contact, the text is handed to the app you choose. That app's own privacy policy applies from there.

Backups

The app opts out of Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

Deleting your data

Clear history from the History tab or turn history off in Settings. Uninstalling removes everything.

Children

Direct is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date, and the in-app Privacy screen will be updated in the next release.
