Direct - Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

Direct is a QR and barcode scanner published by Direct Apps ("we"). This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

Data stored on your device

Scan history, notes and generated codes are stored only in the app's storage on your device. We do not receive them.

We do not require an account, and we do not sell personal data.

Permissions

  • Camera: reads QR codes and barcodes. Frames are processed on the device and not stored or uploaded.
  • Photos and files: used only when you choose an image to scan or save a generated code.
  • Internet and network state: used for in-app purchases and ads, and to open links you choose to visit.

In-app purchases

The Pro upgrade is processed by the Aptoide billing service and the Aptoide Wallet (Aptoide S.A.). Aptoide handles payment details and your Aptoide account. We receive only a purchase confirmation, with no card or bank details. See Aptoide's privacy policy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

This app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Ads may be personalized where you have given consent. In regions where consent is required (such as the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your choice before loading personalized ads, and you can change it from the app's settings. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads). Google's use of data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Third parties

Google AdMob (ads), Aptoide billing (purchases). These providers handle data under their own privacy policies.

Data retention and deletion

Data on your device is kept until you delete it in the app, clear the app's data (Settings > Apps > Direct > Storage > Clear data), or uninstall the app. We hold no data about you on our servers. To ask about deleting ad data held by Google, use Google's controls listed above, or contact us.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. Ads are requested as non-child-directed.

Changes

We will post updates on this page and change the effective date.
